label: Other Antidepressants

[defs]
Atypical and other-mechanism antidepressants, including NDRIs (bupropion), NaSSAs (mirtazapine), SARIs (nefazodone, trazodone), and serotonin modulators (vilazodone, vortioxetine). Note: perphenazine is intentionally excluded — see commented-out entry in generic_names.
