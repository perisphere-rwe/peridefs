condition: diabetes_type1
version: v1
label: Type 1 Diabetes Mellitus
code types: dx_icd9, dx_icd10

[defs: condition]
i: Any of the following:
*: ≥1 inpatient claim with a discharge ICD-9 Type 1 diabetes diagnosis ({.strong 250.x1}, {.strong 250.x3}) or an ICD-10 Type 1 diagnosis (E10.xx) in any discharge position.
*: ≥2 carrier/outpatient claims with the same ICD codes in any position, occurring at least 7 days apart.
*: ≥1 pharmacy claim for insulin or an amylin analogue (see {.strong spec_diabetes_type1}).
